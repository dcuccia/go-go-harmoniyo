🎙️ Gesture → sound latency meter

  1. Open this page on the PC (http://localhost:8080/meter) or any device with a microphone, near where the phone will be knocked.
  2. On the dashboard pick the sound placement (phone or host) and start the Sound-latency test.
  3. Press Record here, then knock the phone flat on the table ~once a second, 20–30 times. The phone (or PC) answers each knock with a 2 kHz beep.
  4. Press Stop & analyse. Delay = beep onset − knock onset (same microphone, same clock). The WAV is uploaded to the host for checking in Audacity.
