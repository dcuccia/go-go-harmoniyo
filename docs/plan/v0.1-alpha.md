## 1. Summary

The alpha is mostly built. The I2 web host already plays 16 carols at 3 difficulties for 1–4
ringers. It has per-ringer highways, timing judged on the phones' source timestamps, a team
score, a seating-chart lobby, and a procedural backing track. The owner played it at home with
a Pixel and said "This is all great, no notes".

What's left for the alpha is **proof and packaging**, not features:

- a real session with 2+ phones;
- proofread carols;
- a deterministic e2e test, with CI that covers the I2 path;
- one-command start;
- help for the certificate warning;
- the release itself.

The holiday release adds **iPhones, 5 ringers, guest-friendly joining, reconnect robustness,
polish, and a how-to-play page**.

**Recommendation:** ship the alpha from `spikes/i2-web-phones` as-is, then graduate the code
into product packages from Nov 16 to 24, before the holiday features
([ADR-0010](../decisions/0010-product-layout.md), option A).

### 6.3 Owner playtest checklist: alpha ([#29](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/29))

About 30 minutes. You need the PC on the TV, 2–5 phones (Pixels; add an iPhone if one is
available), home Wi-Fi, and Node 22. Mark each item ✅ or ❌ and add a note. Post the results in
[#29](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/29).

**Setup**
1. [ ] From a fresh `git pull`, `npm start` at the repo root starts the game ([#25](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/25)).
   The TV shows the lobby and a QR code.
2. [ ] Any start-up check warnings make sense (firewall, adapter, ports, Node version);
   `npm run preflight` runs the checks alone.

**Joining (each phone)**
3. [ ] Scanning the QR opens the certificate warning. The TV help text explains how to get past
   it ([#26](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/26)).
4. [ ] *Tap to start* → allow motion → ringing 6 times calibrates (the banner counts down).
5. [ ] The phone appears in the lobby. Ringing it flashes its chip and lane on the TV.

**Lobby and seating** (with 2+ phones)
6. [ ] Take the part the TV plays from a phone. Swap two phones from a phone (*Swap with …?*)
   and by dragging on the TV.
7. [ ] Keep a part chooser open on the TV while someone presses Ready. It stays open.
8. [ ] Try 🎲 Shuffle, 🔁 Rotate and ⚖️ Balance, then change the song. Seats stay sensible.

**Playing**
9. [ ] Silent Night · Easy · 2 ringers: every phone's bells light its own highway; the next
   zone glows; the bell sounds on the phone.
10. [ ] Jingle Bells · Medium with every phone: hits feel on time. If they're consistently early
    or late, adjust with ←/→ and note the offset.
11. [ ] Turn on the backing track and count-in. The groove sits under the bells and doesn't
    drown them.
12. [ ] Press `` ` `` during play: note the clock-sync error and the ring→TV latency.

**Robustness**
13. [ ] Turn one phone's screen off for about 5 s mid-song: the TV takes its part. Turn it back
    on: the phone gets its part back. (Known: the next-bell glow may not return, [#36](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/36).)

  non-blockers to the next milestone, with a comment.

  milestone.

    and committed.
  - No new asset is missing from `attribution/register.json`.
  - Every `attribution.mjs` warning is resolved or listed under known issues. On 2026-10-24
    there were 13: the 5 carols' proofread reviews ([#30](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/30)), legacy AI provenance gaps for the
    S5 lead sheets and the test fixture, and the palette sheet's CC BY 4.0 confirmation.
  - No YARG or Clone Hero material is in the repository (ADR-0008).

  linked in the notes.
- [ ] The owner has re-run the short checklist on the release candidate (items 1–5, 9–10, 15).

**Tag and publish**
- [ ] Sign off the release commit (`git commit -s`), merge it to `main`, then create the
  annotated tag `git tag -a v0.1.0-alpha -m "Go Go HarmoniYo v0.1.0-alpha"` on the merge commit
  and push it.
- [ ] Publish the GitHub release from the tag, marked **pre-release** for the alpha. The release
  notes cover:
  - **What it is:** one paragraph.
  - **How to play:** clone the repository, run `npm start` at the root (Node.js 22+), scan the QR.
  - **What's in it:** songs (proofread or not), difficulties, ringer counts.

  - **Known issues:** see below.

**Known issues to carry into the alpha notes** (update at release time)
- iPhone not yet supported or tested (holiday: [#34](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/34)). Each phone shows a one-time certificate
  warning ([#26](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/26), [#35](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/35)).
- Phones must be on the same Wi-Fi as the PC; on guest networks, use `--tunnel`.
- Up to 4 ringers (5 in the holiday release, [#33](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/33)). Parts needing more than 4 bells have their
  extra bells rung by the TV.
- On the phone, touching a zone selects a bell and a swing rings it. A tap-to-ring mode is
  under discussion ([#20](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/20)).
- A phone that reconnects mid-song loses the next-bell glow ([#36](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/36)).
- One A/V offset for the whole TV, not per phone ([#37](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/37)).
- Carols marked "unproofread" may contain wrong notes ([#30](https://github.com/dcuccia/go-go-harmoniyo/issues/30)).
- The TV host was tested on Windows with Chrome and Edge only.
